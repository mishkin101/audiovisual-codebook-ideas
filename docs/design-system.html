00

Principles

The interface is a quiet drawing board for what you see. Your visuals bring the color. The interface never does.

  1. Black ground. Every surface starts as void. Nothing glows that doesn't need to.
  2. Monochrome only. Seven steps from black to white. White is reserved for the live moment. The only color ever shown is yours: the colors you assign to moments, drawn as small swatches on the score.
  3. Structure is visible. Hairline rules show the grid, like an architectural drawing. No decoration hides it.
  4. Sharp. Square corners, hard edges. Radius, shadow, blur, gradient, transition.
  5. One typeface. System monospace in a few fixed sizes. Labels are small capitals, data is tabular.
  6. Full bleed. Working pages use the entire display. Chrome recedes while listening.
  7. Time runs left to right. Everything that happens in a track is drawn on one shared horizontal axis.

Ink

Seven tokens, defined in assets/style.css. Use the role, never a raw hex value.

--void#000000ground
--slab#0D0D0Dfilled areas
--rule#262626grid, borders
--dim#4D4D4Dinactive, past passes
--mid#8C8C8Clabels, waveform at rest
--bright#D9D9D9text, played audio
--live#FFFFFFthe now only

The score

A track drawn like a musical score: waveform, ruler, marks and energy share one time axis. Past passes sit underneath in --dim.

06

Controls

Rectangular, hairline, capitals. Active state inverts to white. Fields are a single underline.

07

Layout · Marker

Full display. Header and footer are 44px and fade to --dim while a pass runs. The score fills everything between.
